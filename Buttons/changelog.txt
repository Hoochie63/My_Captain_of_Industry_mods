v1.0.0 | 2026-08-23

[BUTTON MANAGEMENT]
- Buttons discovers native and mod-added controls in the calendar HUD and collects them in one configurable list.
- Enable, hide, reorder, and recolor each button while preserving its original action, icon, tooltip, label, and live state.
- Expand the calendar HUD to the left or right while keeping button order intuitive in either direction.

[PRIMARY AND SECONDARY VIEWS]
- Build two independent button layouts: a compact Primary view and an expanded Secondary view.
- Choose exactly which enabled buttons appear in either view and switch between them with On Click or On Hover mode.
- The active layout and all visibility choices remain global across worlds and complete game restarts.

[ON CLICK MODE]
- Left-click the calendar icon control to switch between Primary and Secondary; right-click it to open settings.
- The last active view is remembered between game sessions.

[ON HOVER MODE]
- Keep Primary visible at rest and reveal Secondary by hovering over the calendar HUD with your mouse.
- Adjust the hover delay so the expanded controls remain available long enough to move between buttons comfortably.
- Hide the calendar icon control from Primary when another Primary button remains visible, while Secondary always preserves settings access.

[SPEED / MOD MODE]
- Use a persistent preset that keeps pause and game-speed controls together while separating mod-menu buttons into the alternate view.
- The preset adapts to your settings; On Click, On Hover, and left or right expansion, while preserving speed-control left to right ordering.
- Any manual visibility or ordering change returns the layout to full user control.

[COLORS AND ORDERING]
- Reorder buttons individually or apply visibility and color changes to the complete list at once.
- Choose colors from a preset palette, enter a hexadecimal value, or fine-tune RGB sliders with live preview.
- Undo the most recent bulk change without disturbing unrelated settings.

[GLOBAL PROFILES]
- Save named snapshots of the complete Buttons configuration and load them to recall all settings and options configured inside buttons setting menu.
- Profiles are stored globally outside the installed mod folder, so they remain available across worlds and reinstalls.


[TWEAKS++ INTEGRATION]
- When Tweaks++ is available, profiles can also restore the saved calendar HUD position using the optional Include position control.
- When installed, title-bar shortcuts open the Tweaks++ HUD page and lock or unlock draggabale huds directly from the Calendar Buttons menu.


[SETTINGS TRAY AND WINDOW]
- Open the collapsible Settings Tray using the gold arrow for profiles, defaults, tooltip control, and the English UI override.
- Pin the settings window, resize it vertically, and optionally enable horizontal resizing for longer button names and translations.
