COI SKYLINES - LINKED CITIES 1.0.4 PUBLIC BETA

Build physically separate Cities while sharing one global Settlement economy.
City Blocks, Town Services, and Settlement Decorations gain flexible placement
without removing their normal gameplay requirements.

LINKED CITIES

Every player City Block belongs to one shared Settlement, even when Cities are
physically separated across the island. City Block inspection panels show the
combined global supply, demand, population, and satisfaction statistics.

SHARED ACROSS LINKED CITIES

- Population and housing capacity
- Food, water, and wastewater
- Electricity
- Household goods and appliances
- Consumer electronics and luxury goods
- Computing / internet
- Hospitals and healthcare
- General waste, recyclables, and biowaste
- Settlement health contributions
- Unity generation and capacity

LINKED TOWN SERVICES

Town Services can be placed anywhere without being attached to a City Block.
They still feed and receive products from the shared Settlement and retain all
normal inputs, outputs, buffers, capacities, needs, satisfaction effects,
workers, power, maintenance, and logistics requirements.

Building more freely does not provide free services. A large population still
needs enough markets, water facilities, hospitals, waste collection, and every
other required service to meet its total demand.

DECONSTRUCTION ANARCHY

Exterior, interior, and bridge City Blocks can be removed even when doing so
splits one visual City into several pieces. Unbuilt City Block plans can also
be removed. Any City Block may be removed while another remains. The final
City Block is protected until all remaining Town Services have been removed.

SNAP-GRID TOGGLE

The Settlement snap-grid can be toggled while placing City Blocks, Town
Services, and Settlement Decorations. Press G or click the C.O.I. Skylines
button in the placement toolbar to switch between snapped and free placement.
Snapping remains available whenever it is useful as an alignment helper.

The shortcut can be changed in the game's Controls screen under
C.O.I. Skylines - Linked Cities (Mod).

DECORATION ANARCHY AND DECORATION RADIUS

Settlement Decorations can be placed away from City Block slots while still
providing their Unity bonus to nearby City Blocks inside their Decoration
Radius. Slightly offset Decorations and small gaps are supported without
extending the bonus into another full City Block ring.

Decorations must still be fully built. Placing several together does not
multiply the bonus received by the same City Block. A Decoration's radius of
influence is recalculated after relevant placement, deconstruction, movement,
rebuild, and load events, so continuous proximity scanning is not needed during
normal gameplay.

RULES THAT REMAIN PHYSICAL AND LOCAL

- Terrain, collision, occupancy, and construction placement rules
- Product buffers, pipes, trucks, and logistics routes
- Per-building workers, maintenance, electricity, and computing
- Each Decoration's physical Decoration Radius

EXISTING SAVES AND REMOVAL

Linked Cities can be added to an existing save. Mature Settlements that have
already accumulated part of a month's health or Unity history may wait until
the next in-game month before their first link.

The mod registers no custom gameplay entities or prototypes and can be removed
from a save. Disabling it does not automatically split City Blocks that were
already combined into one ordinary vanilla Settlement.

PUBLIC BETA NOTES

Decoration Anarchy is in public beta. If any totals differ from an equivalent
vanilla connected City, please report them. Terrain, collision, occupancy,
construction, and Settlement-economy requirements remain active.

SUPPORTED GAME VERSION

Captain of Industry 0.8.6 through 0.8.7a.
