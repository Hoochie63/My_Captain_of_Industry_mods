Concrete Retaining Cube - Changelog

Version 0.4.17

Added localization for every language currently available in Captain of Industry.
Names and descriptions of the standard cube, railing variants, and all three ramp
segments now follow the language selected in the game settings.

Translations are stored as separate JSON files in the Localization folder. They
can be reviewed or corrected without rebuilding the mod. English remains the
fallback if a translation file or an individual entry is missing.

No construction costs, vehicle surfaces, terrain behaviour, models, textures, or
save-game data were changed in this update.
