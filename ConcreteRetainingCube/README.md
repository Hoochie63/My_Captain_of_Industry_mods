# Concrete Retaining Cube

Concrete Retaining Cube adds compact, stackable concrete blocks and ramp segments that use Captain of Industry's retaining-wall construction and terrain behavior.

## Features

- Stackable 1 × 1 × 1 weathered concrete retaining cubes
- Three ramp segments rising from 0% to 33%, 33% to 66%, and 66% to 100%
- Straight, corner, terminating, and T-junction safety-railing variants
- Vehicle surfaces on the tops of cubes and ramps
- Retaining-wall terrain constraints based on the vanilla retaining-wall system
- Standard construction and deconstruction behavior
- Construction cost of 2 concrete slabs and 1 iron per piece
- Unlocks with the vanilla Retaining Walls research
- Available in the vanilla Terraforming toolbar
- Compatibility prototypes for existing saves created with earlier versions
- Localized names and descriptions for all 21 Captain of Industry languages

## How to use

1. Complete the Retaining Walls research.
2. Open the Terraforming construction category.
3. Select a concrete cube, ramp, or railing variant.
4. Place cubes beside or on top of one another to create retaining structures, elevated surfaces, ramps, and protected edges.

## Compatibility and saves

The mod can be added to an existing save.

Do not remove the mod from a save containing any of its cubes or ramps. The manifest intentionally marks the mod as unsafe to remove from an active save.

Verified with Captain of Industry 0.8.6.
