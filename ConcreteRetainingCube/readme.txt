Concrete Retaining Cube 0.4.17
Compatible and verified with Captain of Industry 0.8.6.

Adds stackable 1 x 1 x 1 concrete retaining cubes, railing variants, and three
ramp segments. The pieces use vanilla retaining-wall terrain constraints,
construction behavior, vehicle surfaces, research, and toolbar integration.

The mod can be added to an existing save. Do not remove it from a save containing
its cubes or ramps.
