v1.1.0 | 2026-09-26

Major update:
Regional reputation, trust & exports
====================================
All Beacon & Government+ systems now scale as you increase regional settlements reputation, gain trust through bartering with Captain's Currency and complete repeatable research.
* Captain's currency now displays next to unity in the HUD once some coins have been earned. Left clicking it shows trades & exports (F3), right clicking it opens the goverments edicts (F5). The Coin in HUD can be disabled in F3 → Regional Exports Tab And F5 Edict screen now has 2 tabs for Beacon Policies & Regional Subsidies.
* Added Captain's Currency, Beacon Network Unity, regional reputation and trust readouts where relevant in F5 and F3 Menus and building inspectors.
* Kayser's Pre-Industrial Era  Tier-0 Lighthouse keeps its own refugee and supply rewards; Beacon & Government rewards and Captain's Currency begin only after the normal Beacon is constructed and no longer overwrite pre-Industrial's intended beacon reward progression.

[REGIONAL EXPORTS]
* Added Regional Export Yards tier I-IV, with 250/500/750/1,000 units of intake-only storage per unlocked beacon reward. New Yards begin with truck intake disabled, so food and other goods are opt-in.
* Each explored settlement independently may request up to three products every three years. Dispatch a repaired Cargo Ship to fulfill an order for Captain's Currency. Any settlement can receive one delivery per request period.
* Added a native F3 Regional exports overview with settlement browsing, demand, fulfillment and direct Yard access.
* Payouts depend on item quality and quantities sent. Send Minimum (preserves surplus), Current stock (sends all stored requested goods if minimums are met), or Maximum (waits until all requested storages are full). Optional export automation follows the same mode; researching the next Yard tier unlocks automation for the previous tier.
* Added per-product intake controls; Choose Enable All/Disable All/Request only or manual selection; trucks will only deliver enabled products.
* Sort storage by name/rarity/quantity/category, storage filters, use a six-row expanded view, and review export history for the list 20 orders. Deconstructed yard-contents are queued for recovery to the primary Shipyard, including when none is currently available.

[GOVERNMENT, REPUTATION & TRUST]
* Completed village-reputation levels add up to +25% to Beacon rewards and Captain's Exchange Quick Trade quantities, increase regional aid-request chance from 50% to at most 75%, and discount subsidies by up to 25%.
* Added Regional Trust: each Captain's Currency coin earned or spent grants one permanent trust. Post-Government Office I research now requires increasing Regional trust to continue unlocking later research, starting at 100 trust; Use Beacon Network, quick trades, and later, exports & subsidies to build your trust with other regions.
* Adds one new Beacon reward policy to redirect immigrants for 5% more product reward after each beacon cycle and four new emergency subsidies: garbage, water, wastewater & unity. They're expensive, but can help prevent death-spiral crisis if you've been a good neighbor. Future updates will further reduce cost as trust increases.
* Every five regional-reputation levels unlock multiple purchase scaling options for Quick Trade and subsidies, reaching 5× at +20 reputation levels. Scale multiplies subsidy capacity or quick trades quantities and purchase price for bigger one-time purchases.
* Moved the former standalone policy window into native F5 Captain's Office menu: Beacon Policies tab and Regional Subsidies tab.
* Added repeatable Power Subsidy Capacity research after the Regional Power Agreements node. It requires five completed village-reputation levels and 2000 Regional Trust to start, each level increases Power Generation Subsidy output by 25% per level but is not required to continue researching further nodes. Regional Trust requirements rise by 250 per level, uses extreme repeat research cost type scaling and caps at level 20.

[ITEM QUALITY]
* Added Product quality colors to all beacon rewards to show their rarity and relative value, shown in Captain's Exchange quick trades and Export Yard.
* Added configurable rarity-filtered Quick Trade notification alerts, off by default. When on, a notification will appear anytime your selected rarity appears for quick trade in a captain's exchange.
* Added new epic and legendary quick trades. 1000 coin Cargo Ship with permenant price increase after each purchase, Instant 100 Unity, which cannot go over your unity cap and Instant research points, which scale depending on your current research progression.
* Added fuel gas and hydrogen to all Beacon+ systems and adjusted some beacon network reward & quick trades quantities and export sell prices based on a 33% buy/sell value, not including reputation gains buffs. With a fully unlocked reputation, trust and repeatable research in future B&G+ updates, quick trades can become profitable, while limited.

* Full translations audits across all 21 supported languages.

OTHER MOD COMPATIBILITY

* All data read and write is saved to localized snapshots and recalled safely to avoid enumeration failures with other mods.

v1.0.0 | 2026-07-27 - 2026-08-26
[BEACON & GOVERNMENT DLC - USE MORE UNITY TO GUIDE SHIPS BACK HOME]
* Designed to keep the Beacon useful and engaging into the mid-to-late game without becoming overpowered or cheaty.
* This is an early but working release, with more updates planned for the immediate future.

[REWARDS, CURRENCY & TRADING]
* Overhauls the Beacon's fixed reward of 18 refugees and 20 Copper into a weighted, randomized supply drop after every completed Beacon cycle.
* Rewards draw from a large research-gated pool of ores, metals, fuels, foods, construction supplies, electronics, medical goods, Solar Cells, Vehicle Parts, Lab Equipment, and other useful products.
* Reward quantities vary and scale from the Beacon network's saved full-cycle average Unity drain, preventing last-second toggle cheating.
* A rare multi-reward roll can add one to four extra product stacks, becoming slightly more likely at each whole 1.00 Unity/month average threshold up to the cap.
* Beacon rewards are designed as a minor supplement so village trading and contracts remain the better sources for focused supplies.

[CAPTAIN'S CURRENCY]
* Captain's Currency is earned only through completed Beacon cycles, with the amount based on the network's saved full-cycle average Unity drain.
* Each Beacon cycle awards roughly one Coin for every 1.00 Unity/month of full-cycle average, with randomized variance.
* Build a Captain's Exchange to access three yearly rotating offers that cost Captain's Currency. Purchased products are delivered to the Shipyard.

[BEACON NETWORK & BUILDINGS]
* Adds Relay Beacons, Captain's Exchanges, and Government Offices I and II using the Beacon, Trading Dock, and Captain's Office models.
* These support buildings can be armed to increase how much Unity the primary Beacon drains each month.
* Armed support buildings strengthen Beacon rewards only while the primary Beacon operates. Their Unity drain stops while it is paused or unconstructed.
* Relay Beacons cost 0.25 Unity/month, Office I costs 1.00, and Office II costs 1.50. They can also be left disarmed and used decoratively without participating in the Unity-drain mechanic.
* Added global Arm/Disarm controls and camera shortcuts for jumping to the primary Beacon or cycling through Beacon network buildings.
* All custom buildings are available from the dedicated Beacon & Government logistics toolbar menu.
* Armed support buildings show a small contextual indicator while their inspector is open.

[COMPATIBILITY WITH COASTAL IMMIGRATION BEACON]
* Fixed Coastal Immigration Beacon v0.2.10's oversized 60+ tile placement restriction conflict, restoring the normal 5x5 Beacon placement rules so Beacon-derived content no longer inherits its bug.
