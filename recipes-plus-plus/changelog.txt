v1.2.1 | 2026-08-12
* Now supports game version 0.8.7a.

* Realistic water electrolysis now makes half as much per batch and uses 4.2x power on both Electrolyzer tiers. (thanks antonio220 for the balance suggestion)

* The Crude oil, Diesel and Naphtha recipes made from Liquified coal now sit in the Liquifier instead of the Chemical plant. 
   Also moved Synthetic diesel to the Liquifier. Keeping base game buildings free of mod recipes and makes more sense to live there.

  - They also make less per batch, so they stay a nice extra for spare coal rather than a stronger replacement or as a second extra recipe for the base game's oil chain.

* The Liquid coal generator now puts out 5 MW instead of 3 MW while burning 16 Liquified coal a minute instead of 24, making it worth building as a peak power buffer.

* Synthetic diesel now needs only 2 sulfur per batch instead of 6. (thanks antonio220 for the suggestion)

  - The Synt generator now burns 25 synthetic diesel a minute instead of 30 for the same 3.8 MW output, so every unit of fuel goes further. (thanks antonio220 for the suggestion)

* The Battery plant now charges 6 cells a minute instead of 4 every 90 seconds, and uses 6x power instead of 8x, so charged cells cost about a third of the power they used to and one plant keeps up with a lot more vehicles.

  - Charging cells no longer needs any computing at all, and assembling them now needs 8 TFlops instead of 15. Building the cells is the part that needs computers, not pushing current into them. (thanks antonio220 for the suggestion)

* The charging station now hands back 60% of the batteries it uses instead of 70%, so you still need to build new cells over time rather than recharging the same ones forever.
