Blueprint Products Info
=======================

Shows which products buildings in a blueprint consume and produce.

Open the blueprints window, select a blueprint, and scroll the detail panel:
- "Inputs" — products consumed by buildings in the blueprint
- "Outputs" — products produced by buildings in the blueprint

Install: copy the mod folder to %APPDATA%/Captain of Industry/Mods/

The mod includes Thumbnail.png — icon shown in the game's mod list.
