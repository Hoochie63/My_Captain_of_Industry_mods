v0.3.0 | 20026-09-05
Active Flow Control, Alarms & Localisation

* NEW: Flow Regulation – Set a target flow rate and ability to automatically limit throughput between the meter's input and output.
* NEW: SCADA Alarms – Added configurable Low/High Flow alarms linked to the game's notification system. Set limits from below 0% to 75% and above from 100% to 200% of the target.
* NEW: Localisation – Added Russian, French, Simplified Chinese, Greek, German, Spanish, and Brazilian Portuguese. Translation .json files are included for community edits and new languages.
* NEW: Regulator UI – Added flow targets, regulation toggle, and alarm settings to the HMI Inspector.
* IMPROVED: Auto Setpoints – New or loaded meters automatically set the target flow to the maximum throughput of connected conveyors.
* IMPROVED: Pause & Power Handling – The internal timer now correctly stops during game pauses, broken maintenance states, and power loss. Historical charts no longer incorrectly drop to zero.
* IMPROVED: Smarter Alarms – Added 2% hysteresis to stop alarms flickering around the limit. Notifications now show the actual product being transported.

v0.2.0 | 2026-07-12
Elevated Placement & History Data

* NEW: Added elevated placement support. The Flowmeter can now be built in the air on multi-level factory designs with automatic pillar spawning.
* NEW: Added dual-chart visualization (activated on icon hover) to display both Monthly and Annual average throughput histories.
* NEW: Completely overhauled the inspector into a professional HMI-style dashboard featuring a custom floating UI widget for the chart.
* NEW: Implemented interactive, scaling bar charts that display exact values when hovering over the bars.
* IMPROVED: Replaced legacy exponential smoothing with strict discrete buffers for 12-month and 12-year historical data.
* IMPROVED: Implemented a "Sliding Window Blend" algorithm to smoothly crossfade data at the start of a new month or year, ensuring accurate one-month and one-year averages in the inspector window.
* FIX: Ensured 100% legacy compatibility with old savegames.
