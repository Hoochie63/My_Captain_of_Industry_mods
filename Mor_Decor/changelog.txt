v1.0.2 | 2026-07-30
* Fixed an issue where loading a save with overlapping Mor Decor props could prevent removed debris from disappearing visually.
* Existing affected saves are cleaned automatically when loaded with this version.
* Added Decorative Landscaping research for Mulch and Decorative Rock production.
* Added descriptions to the Foliage Decor placement tools.

v1.0.0 | 2026-07-24
* Initial prerelease of Mor Decor.
* Adds mulch as a loose product.
* Mulch can be used as a terrain material for dumping and placement.
* Adds a shredder recipe that produces mulch from wood. (WIP)
* Adds decorative terrain props using existing in-game assets. No research required. (WIP)
* Adds placeable bushes, flowers, grass, rocks, and debris-style decorations.
* [CTRL] + [Scroll-Wheel] to rescale decorations.
* [Shift] + [Scroll-Wheel] to cycle different decoration variants.
* Adds a dedicated foliage category in the toolbar's Forestry menu.
* Supports adding the mod to existing saved games.
* Not remove-safe: Removal has not been tested. Foliage decorations use vanilla assets, while the mulch terrain does not.
