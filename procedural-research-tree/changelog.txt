v0.3.2 | 2026-10-05
* Remembers your selected layout, research filter, opacity, and prerequisite-navigation setting between game launches.
* Keeps the active choice checked in every research-tree dropdown after restarting the game.
* Adds a right-click shortcut on the Layers button to show all research immediately.
* Labels Compact 3 as the PRT default so it is not confused with the original authored layout.

v0.3.1 | 2026-09-16
* Verifies compatibility with Captain of Industry v0.8.7d.
* A special thanks to SirColibri, author of Colibri Industries, for helping brainstorm ideas during this mod's development. Now our overlapping research nodes are close cousins, once removed = )
* Adds Colibri Industries as an optional mod.
* Corrects the opacity slider display so its label matches the actual 0%-50% opacity range while highlighting nodes.
* Left-clicking to close the Filter Menu maintains it's gold coloring—indicating a filter is still active.

v0.3.0 | 2026-09-15
* Adds editable English-key translations for all 21 supported game languages.
* Adds small research-window controls for all features.
* Adds alternative layout options, including Original Vanilla Layout to rebuild the research tree live, without needing to restart.
* Adds clickable REQUIRED BY details for complete two-way tree navigation.
* Adds Mod Highlighting to identify nodes belonging to a selected mod: Filter by 1 mod, all mods, or vanilla-only.
* 0%-50% opacity slider appears only while filtering is active; Dims all other nodes.
* Adds PRT_TREE console command for logging the auto layout's result for use in reporting issues.
* Kept some testing layouts: Compact, Nearest, and Packed layouts with spacing 1 through 4; Compact 3 is the PRT default, while spacing of 1 or 2 allows some overlap to occur in exchange for less overall height. More improvements to layout can be made as needed/suggested.

NOTES: If an author patches their own REQUIRED or REQUIRED BY Details, then PRT skips modifying that node's details to avoid duplications. This does not guarantee an author adds their own click-navigation.

v0.1.0b | 2026-09-13
* Public no-Harmony alternative
* Adaptive-layout foundation.
* Keeps the vanilla research tree fixed as the progression backbone.
* Detects connected per-mod research branches and preserves their authored shape.
* Moves colliding branches only by deterministic four-grid-unit vertical offsets.
* Repairs malformed overlaps inside a connected branch without changing horizontal progression.
* Runs after all normal research prototypes are registered and again before the research window builds.
* Never changes prerequisites, research costs, unlocks, conditions, or saved research state.
