FILTER RESEARCH BY MOD
======================
Discover which nodes belong to which mod to quickly find a mod's research. Filter by 1 mod, all mods, or vanilla-only.
* 0%-50% opacity slider that appears only while filtering; Nodes not part of the filter are dimmed.

REQUIRED BY NAVIGATION
======================
* Adds clickable REQUIRED BY details for two-way tree navigation. 

CONSOLE COMMAND
===============
Use PRT_TREE in the console to log your exact tree's layout to report any layout issues

FULL LANGUAGE SUPPORT
=====================
* Adds editable English-key translations for all 21 supported game languages.

NOTES: Changes only research-tree coordinates and presentation; prerequisites, costs, unlocks, and progression are never changed.
If an author patches their own REQUIRED or REQUIRED BY Details, then PRT skips modifying that node's details to avoid duplications. This does not guarantee an author adds their own click-navigation.

* Public no-Harmony alternative
* Adaptive-layout foundation.
* Keeps the vanilla research tree fixed as the progression backbone.
* Detects connected per-mod research branches and preserves their authored shape.
* Moves colliding branches only by deterministic four-grid-unit vertical offsets.
* Repairs malformed overlaps inside a connected branch without changing horizontal progression.
* Runs after all normal research prototypes are registered and again before the research window builds.
* Never changes prerequisites, research costs, unlocks, conditions, or saved research state.
* Removes the Harmony and Mafi.Unity dependencies from the layout implementation.
