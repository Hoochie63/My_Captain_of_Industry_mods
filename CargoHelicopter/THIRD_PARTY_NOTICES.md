## Sikorsky S-64 Skycrane helicopter model

Helicopter model based on **“Sikorsky S-64 Skycrane”** by **helijah**, licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Cockpit interior contribution by **Wayne Bragg**.

Original model:
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/sikorsky-s-64-skycrane-e33d6c36c6fd4268a6759a171564dbc1

The model was modified, optimized, retextured, and adapted for use in the Cargo Helicopter mod
for Captain of Industry.

The original low-poly helicopter base/reference mesh was published by **drummyfish** as part of
[32 Low Poly Models](https://opengameart.org/content/32-low-poly-models) under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The aircraft shipped by this mod
has since been extensively rebuilt and reworked; the original attribution is retained for provenance.

Captain of Industry and its assemblies are not redistributed by this package. They are referenced
only from the user's game installation during compilation and at runtime.
