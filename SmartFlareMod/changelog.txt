* Improved the panel help text and refined Russian storage-size grammar.
* Fixed storage size labels: Small (200), Medium (500), Large (3,000), and Very Large (6,000).
* Storage selection is now sorted by distance, with the nearest storage shown first.
* Added in-game UI translations for every language currently shipped with Captain of Industry.
* Smart Flare text now follows the language selected by the player.
* Smart Flare settings now work with Captain of Industry's standard copy/paste settings tool and blueprints.
* Copies the selected storage plus the minimum and maximum thresholds.
