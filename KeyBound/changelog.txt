v1.0.0 | 2026-08-23

Ω—KEYBOUND—Ω Successor to Not Enough Keybinds.

[LIVE REBIND]

- Click Toolbar or Calendar Buttons to quickly Rebind them without needing to access escape menu or browse long keybind lists.
- This works even for mod buttons that didn't have a bind option.
- Shows available NEK & Controls binds and related conflicts so everything can be fixed without needing to go into escape menu.
- Open Live Rebind from the Bind Manager's Green Shortcut in the Titlebar, Shift-click or Ctrl-click the floating NEK button UI, or with a keybind. Rebind Mode under KeyBound -> Misc. Binds -> KeyBound Binds.
- To select a button: right-click it or Shift or Ctrl modified-Left or Right clicks.
 
[FRAMEWORK INTEGRATION & KEYBINDS]

- Automatically discovers supported vanilla and mod-added menus, submenus, tools, items, buildings, UI panels, and Calendar Buttons.
- Organizes them under the KeyBound Framework tab with Bind 1, Bind 2, and left- or right-side modifier support.
- Active shortcuts appear on toolbar Menu's hover Keybind-labels.
- Save & Load all existing keybinds both in Controls, and the Framework Tab Controls for KeyBound
- Create profiles from your current controls and KeyBound-framework binds.
- First-time setup creates a protected Backup of you controls to prevent losing your keybinds before experimenting with KeyBound or Built-In Profiles.
- Controls, NEK & BindProfiles file path: C:\Users\[User]\AppData\Roaming\Captain of Industry\ModConfigs\not-enough-keybinds\Profiles
- Left-click the K Calendar Button or floating NEK button for the compact Controller; right-click opens the full Keybind Manager.
- Apply saved profiles or use ready-made choices including Toolbar Mode, focused item layouts, and Mag's All-In-One.

- Includes English and some translations for all 20 additional supported game languages, plus an English UI option in the Manager's Tooltip settings.
- Supports Captain of Industry 0.8.5 through 0.8.7b, Keybind Framework 2.0.2 or newer, and existing saves.
