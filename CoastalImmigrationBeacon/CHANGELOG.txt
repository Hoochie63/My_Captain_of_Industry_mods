Coastal Immigration Beacon

Changelog v0.2.15
- The vanilla Immigration Beacon now requires a valid coastal ocean berth.

Changelog v0.2.14
- Added a visible pier and docking-area placement preview.
- Added an animated immigrant boat with arrival, docking, and departure sequences.
- Added ship engine sounds, horns, smoke, and water effects.
- Added a status panel showing whether the coastal berth is valid.
- Added localization for 21 languages.
- Existing inland Beacons remain in saved games, but immigration pauses until they are rebuilt on the coast.
- The boat is purely visual and does not affect fleet limits, immigration rewards, or save files.
- Compatible with existing saved games.
- Requires Captain of Industry v0.8.6.
