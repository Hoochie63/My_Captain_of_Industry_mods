v1.0.0 | 2026-08-23

===================
TOWER UI SETTINGS
===================
* Keep heavily modded Mining Tower inspectors compact, readable, and selectable.
* Control Excavators, Trucks, Materials/Disposal, and Import/Export without letting long lists push content off-screen.

==================
ROWS AND SCROLLING
==================
* Choose one to eight visible rows for each controlled section.
* Scrollbars appear only when content exceeds the limit, and both halves of split sections scroll independently.

==================
VEHICLE VISIBILITY
==================
* Filter excavators and trucks automatically by current ownership, by fuel type, or by individual vehicle.
* Preserve the game's research and availability rules while updating every open Mining Tower immediately.

===========================
COLLAPSE AND DEFAULT STATES
===========================
* Collapse every section at once or reopen only the four standard sections.
* Each Mining Tower remembers its collapsed sections.
* Choose which standard and Auto Terrain Designations sections start open on newly placed Mining Towers.

==============================
COMPATIBILITY AND LOCALIZATION
==============================
* Works with Gameplay++ and Auto Terrain Designations without duplicating their collapse controls.
* Uses live localized game and mod names and includes all 21 game languages.
* Verified for Captain of Industry 0.8.7b and safe to add to or remove from existing saves.
